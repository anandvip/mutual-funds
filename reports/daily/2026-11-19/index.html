Glossary
AMFI
Association of Mutual Funds in India, the official NAV source.
CAGR / growth per year (5y)
Compound annual growth rate over the trailing 5 years, to the latest NAV. This is a per-year average, not the total 5-year gain.
Current manager
The manager’s years on the fund, and the growth per year and worst fall during that tenure. On this page, years are the 19 Nov report’s matched-history figures. Tenure growth and fall are not stated there, and are marked “from 20 Nov report”.
Confidence tier
High, Medium or Low trust in a fund’s verdict, set by the length of matched history, whether it has been tested by a real fall, and any driver-map flags. Under 5 years of matched history caps a fund at Medium. A fund never tested by a real fall is capped at Low. A driver-map red flag lowers the tier by one.
Days to recover / days under water
Days from the peak before the worst fall until NAV got back to that peak.
Direct Growth
The direct plan, growth option: no distributor commission, and returns stay in the fund instead of being paid out.
Driver map
A note of what tends to move a fund (rates, flows, a sector cycle, and so on), back-checked against its past rises and falls, with the strength labelled strong, some or weak. It can step a confidence tier down; it does not change rank.
Drop candidate
A fund that is on the watchlist but is failing the pass marks, with a formal drop decision due on the drop-check date (8 Dec for the original funds).
Fund history
Growth per year and the worst fall as an investor in the fund experienced them, over the fund’s own record (here, 5-year growth per year and the worst fall over the whole history). This view is kept separate from the current-manager view.
Governance hold
A verdict that pauses a fund pending a regulatory or fund-house issue, whatever its numbers. Quant Large & Mid Cap is labelled “Watch, governance hold”.
Star
A fund whose numbers look strong enough to stand out, while it is still under observation (also called an emerging star).
Verdict · confidence
The verdict and the confidence tier shown in one cell, for example Star · Low. A strong verdict with Low confidence is not a buy signal.
Growth-to-fall ratio
5-year growth per year % ÷ |worst fall %|. On this page the ratio uses the worst fall over the fund’s whole history.
Keep
A fund that passes the current marks and stays under observation.
Matched-mandate history
The part of a fund’s record that matches its current mandate and current lead manager. From 19 Nov this starts on the lead manager’s start date, pending a rule question.
MDD / worst fall
Maximum drawdown: the worst peak-to-trough fall in NAV, from daily NAVs. On this page that is the worst fall over the full direct-plan history.
NAV
Net asset value per unit.
Nifty 50 proxy
UTI Nifty 50 Index Direct (scheme code 120716), used as the Nifty 50 reference.
SEBI
Securities and Exchange Board of India, the regulator.
Skew
Skewness of monthly returns. Positive means a fatter upside tail. SBI Focused fails this check against its category.
TER
Total expense ratio, the annual fund cost.
Best / 2nd-best / weakest year
In the big-years table, the highest, second-highest and lowest calendar-year returns in 2021–2025, taken from the report’s calendar-year table. A weakest year shown with a + means the fund had no losing year in that window.
Year to date / YTD
The calendar-year return so far in 2026, as stated in the report, to the latest NAV (18 Nov 2026, or 17 Nov for DSP Value).
Top-2 share
The share of 2021–2025 compounded growth that came from the two best calendar years, on a log basis. High means growth from one or two boom years; low means steadier compounding.
Unverified / unproven-stress rule
If the matched-mandate history never saw a category fall of 10% or more, tail protection is Unverified and confidence is capped at Low until the fund survives its first real stress event. That applies to DSP Value on this page.
v1.1 pass marks
A fund passes if it meets at least 3 of 4 marks: skew above the category median; a 10% tail ratio above the Nifty 50’s 1.25; upside capture above downside capture; and an Ulcer Index below the category median.
Watch
A fund under closer review. It may still pass the marks, but something is weak, untested, or new.

Pillar method (working thresholds, pending approval: see Proposal B)

  • A, Tail protection: the fund's worst fall and worst 12 months, compared with the median of same-category peers over the same window. Strong = better on both. Weak = more than 5 points worse on either. Otherwise Mixed. One step down if recovery took more than twice as long as the peer median. Unverified if the matched-mandate period never saw a category fall of 10% or more.
  • B, Rolling consistency: the share of rolling 3-year and 5-year windows in which the fund beat its category's median index. Strong 65% or more, Weak under 45%.
  • C, Right-tail convexity: four checks: skew above the category median, tail ratio above 1.25, best 12 months above the peer median, and strong-rally capture above 1.0 (Nifty months above +4%). Strong = 3 or 4 checks.
  • D, Structural stability: manager tenure, AUM size for the category, TER and governance (sourced below).
  • Tier: High = 3 or more Strong and no Weak. Low = 2 or more Weak. Otherwise Medium. Then the rulebook caps: under 5 years matched → max Medium; never stress-tested → max Low; driver red flag → one step down.
  • Matched-mandate start = the current lead manager's start date (strict reading of the rulebook; see Proposal A). Sources: Bandhan AMFI SSD_12077 (Gunwani, 28 Jan 2023); Canara AMFI SSD_1582 (Mishra, 26 Jun 2021); BOI Mfg & Infra AMFI SSD_4457 (Gosar, 27 Sep 2022); ICICI Pru Mid Cap factsheet (Lalit Kumar, 8 Aug 2022); ICICI Pru Focused factsheet (Dusad, 8 Aug 2022); SBI Focused factsheet Dec 2025 (Srinivasan, May 2009); Quant Large & Mid: Quant factsheet Oct 2026 via search (Ankit Pande, 10 May 2020; Tandon primary from 3 Feb 2025). Others are carried from the 17–18 Nov reports.
  • Category stress: taken from a monthly category-median index built from the funds in our screen. Month-end data understates falls within the month.
