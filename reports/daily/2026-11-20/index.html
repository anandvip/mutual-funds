Glossary
AMFI
Association of Mutual Funds in India, the official NAV source.
Amber (driver map)
A risk noted in a driver map that is not strong enough to be a red flag; it does not change the tier.
CAGR / growth per year (5y)
Compound annual growth rate over the trailing 5 years, to the latest NAV. This is a per-year average, not the total 5-year gain.
Current manager
The manager’s years on the fund, and the growth per year and worst fall during that tenure only. For SBI Healthcare (Desai, since Jun 2011) and SBI Focused (Srinivasan, since May 2009), the Direct-plan record from 2 Jan 2013 is used, because the managers started before Direct plans existed. Quant Large & Mid Cap is counted from 3 Feb 2025, with Tandon as primary.
Confidence tier
High, Medium or Low trust in a fund’s verdict, set by the pillars, the length of matched history, stress exposure and driver-map flags. Under 5 years of matched history caps a fund at Medium. A fund never tested by a real fall is capped at Low. A driver-map red flag lowers the tier by one.
Driver map
A per-fund note of what tends to move it (rates, crude, flows, and so on), back-checked against its past rises and falls, with the strength labelled strong, some or weak. A driver map can step a confidence tier down; it never changes rank.
Fund history
The fund’s whole Direct-plan record: growth per year (5-year average) and the worst fall over that whole history, as an investor in the fund experienced them. Never mixed with the current-manager view.
Governance hold
A verdict that pauses a fund pending a regulatory or fund-house issue, whatever its numbers. Quant Large & Mid Cap is labelled “Watch, governance hold”.
Growth-to-fall ratio
5-year growth per year ÷ the absolute value of the worst fall. The main table uses the worst fall over full history.
Keep
Stay on the watchlist and keep observing.
Matched-mandate history
The part of a fund’s record that matches its current mandate and current lead manager. From 19 Nov, it starts on the lead manager’s start date, which is still one of the rule questions with Vipul (Proposal A).
Policy-driven theme
A fund whose holdings share one driver set by government action (for example PSU funds: elections, government share sales), so they rise and fall together even across sectors.
Real fall in tenure
Whether the fund’s category fell 10% or more (peak to trough, month-end category median index) while the current manager was in charge. “No” means the manager is untested. On 20 Nov this was yes for 13 of 14 funds; DSP Value is the exception.
Star
Emerging star: a keep-list fund with a strong tail profile. A Star verdict with Low confidence is not a buy signal.
Survival flag
Triggered if the worst fall or worst 12 months is more than 15 points worse than the Nifty 50. All 14 funds pass on 20 Nov.
Tail protection Unverified
The matched-mandate history never saw a category fall of 10% or more, so tail protection is Unverified and confidence is capped at Low. DSP Value stays in this state.
Top-2 share
The share of a fund’s 2021–2025 compounded growth that came from its two best calendar years (log basis). High means growth from one or two boom years; low means steadier compounding.
Verdict · confidence
The verdict and the confidence tier shown in one cell, for example Star · Low. A strong verdict with Low confidence is not a buy signal.
Watch
Stay on the list, but with a clearer concern than a Keep.
